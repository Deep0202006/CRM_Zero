# Distributor Status Contract

## CURRENT

Distributor Status is the operational lifecycle and renewal sub-domain beneath Payment Collections. `distributor_accounts.distributor_id` is the single account identity. Optional Lead linkage and exact Receivables identity references never transfer mutation authority.

## AUTHORITY

- Distributor Status owns installation, training, mapping, activity, operational billing indication, assigned employee, renewal date, canonical `erp_id`, nullable operational `erp_payment_status`, version, and immutable operational events. ERP identity resolves exactly through `public.erp_systems`; readers never fuzzy-match it or copy it into Renewal or financial tables. ERP payment status is a manual operational ERP-settlement fact, not financial paid/received/outstanding truth, and may be set only while canonical Receivables derives `PAID`.
- Receivables exclusively owns invoice amounts, balances, reported/confirmed payments, reversals, and financial events. `billing_status=billed` creates no financial row.
- Facts are orthogonal. Mapping is `pending`/`done` for current records and nullable only for historical rows where it was never captured. No historical mapping state or date is fabricated.
- Mapped means installation, training, and mapping are done. Active/Inactive and Billed remain overlapping projections and do not imply Mapped.
- Renewal reminders derive from the one PostgreSQL `renewal_date`: T-2, T-1, today, and overdue, and only operationally billed Distributor accounts participate. No Task, reminder row, Pipeline follow-up, Call, or polling is created.
- Payment Collection Renewals is a read/edit experience over that same field. Its four urgency cards are derived against the IST business date and never become stored status.

## IDENTITY AND IMPORT

Mutable names are not primary identity. A unique distributor reference is preferred; name-only preview is conservative and becomes ambiguous when multiple matches exist. Preview writes nothing. Commit reconstructs authority server-side, revalidates mapping and all other facts, uses a stable operation/hash, and commits atomically. XLSX/XLS/CSV are bounded to 10 MB and 5,000 rows; spreadsheet bytes are never stored.

The unified master import has one deterministic `CRM_DISTRIBUTOR_MASTER_V2` XLSX contract and the exact filename `ZeroData_Distributor_Master_Import.xlsx`. Its sheets, in exact order, are `Distributors`, `Receivables`, `Payments`, and `Instructions`. Every sheet remains present, and an unused business sheet retains its canonical header row. At least one business row must exist across the three business sheets. The workbook is limited to 10 MB, 5,000 rows per business sheet, and 5,000 business rows total. Unknown, missing, blank, or duplicate headers and any formula cell reject the complete workbook. Users must paste literal values only; a formula is never evaluated or converted during import. A structural V1 workbook fails as `MASTER_TEMPLATE_OUTDATED` and directs the user to download the latest template.

The exact `Distributors` columns are `Distributor Reference`, `ERP`, `Distributor Name`, `Assigned Employee Email`, `Installation Status`, `Installation Date`, `Training Status`, `Training Date`, `Mapping Status`, `Mapped Date`, `Activity Status`, `Billing Status`, `Bill Date`, `Operational Bill Reference`, and `Renewal Date`. The exact `Receivables` columns are `Distributor Reference`, `Bill Reference`, `Contact Person`, `Contact Phone`, `Bill Amount`, `Bill Due Date`, `Payment Follow-up Date`, and `Notes`. The exact `Payments` columns are `Distributor Reference`, `Bill Reference`, `Payment Import Key`, `Payment Amount`, `Payment Date`, `Payment Mode`, `Payment Reference`, and `Notes`.

Realistic literal examples are: Distributor `ZD-MUM-001 | Mumbai Central Distributor | owner@example.com | done | 2026-08-01 | done | 2026-08-02 | done | 2026-08-03 | active | billed | 2026-08-04 | OPS-2026-001 | 2027-08-04`; Receivable `ZD-MUM-001 | INV-2026-001 | Priya Shah | +91 98765 43210 | 84500.00 | 2026-09-01 | 2026-08-25 | Annual platform fee`; Payment `ZD-MUM-001 | INV-2026-001 | ERP-RCPT-0042 | 4500.50 | 2026-08-19 | Bank Transfer | UTR-0042 | Historical confirmed receipt`. These examples are documentation in `Instructions`, not preloaded business rows. Text is Unicode-normalized, trimmed, and whitespace-collapsed; emails and controlled statuses are case-normalized; accepted Indian/ISO dates become `YYYY-MM-DD`; money becomes an exact two-decimal string.

`Distributor Reference` is required in every unified data row and is the stable cross-sheet distributor identity. `Distributor Reference + Bill Reference` targets one exact Receivable. A Payment also requires a stable `Payment Import Key`, scoped later by its resolved exact Receivable, so historical confirmed payment replay can be idempotent. Workbook parsing neither writes business data nor treats operational `Billing Status` as a Receivable.

Master Distributor resolution never falls back to mutable name matching. It derives the canonical `code:<normalized reference>` identity from the required case-insensitive `Distributor Reference`, resolves existing accounts in bounded 100-key reads, and resolves assignment through the certified exact Auth-login-email/active-internal-non-Admin employee directory. New account UUIDs are stable from the master operation plus normalized reference; updates bind the canonical `distributor_id` and `expected_version`. New Distributor rows require ERP. For an existing account, a blank cell means `NO_CHANGE`; blank ERP preserves the assignment and `[CLEAR]` intentionally removes it. Other `[CLEAR]` support remains limited to documented nullable fields. The resulting complete lifecycle state must remain valid. Missing employees, incomplete new rows, repeated references, contradictory identity, stale version, or concurrent uniqueness changes fail closed.

ERP Partner Viewer is an exclusive external capability. Dedicated service-only projections authorize every row by active user, capability, and `erp_partner_scopes`; they expose operational Distributor facts, the operational ERP paid/not-paid status, and billed-only derived Renewal context. They never expose internal employee identity, notes, Receivable/Payment identifiers, amounts, references, history, or mutation controls. ERP Partner accounts are not eligible employees and cannot participate in attendance, internal synchronization, or operational assignments.

Unified preview is one write-free cross-sheet resolution pass. It shares the bounded certified employee directory, resolves stable Distributor UUIDs first, overlays those identities into Receivables, then overlays exact Receivable UUIDs into Payments. Every normalized data row receives one of its sheet's closed classification values, an explicit `CREATE`, `UPDATE`, `CONFIRM`, `SKIP`, or `BLOCK` action, canonical Before state, and complete proposed After state; Payment After states are cumulative across every planned event for the exact Receivable. A blocking Distributor prevents dependent financial rows from falling back to another account. The canonical resolved-plan SHA-256 binds the format, operation, IST business date, classifications, actions, Before/After authority, exact Distributor/Receivable/Payment UUIDs, and mutation payloads. Confirmation accepts no client-supplied execution plan: it reparses the same uploaded workbook bytes, rebuilds current authority, and accepts only the identical hash. Any changed version, identity, employee, bill, balance, payment event, action, or business date returns HTTP 409 `IMPORT_REFRESH_REQUIRED` with a replacement preview.

Atomic confirmation is one service-only Migration 046 RPC and one PostgreSQL transaction. The outer operation serializes by operation UUID, persists one master receipt, and invokes the existing Distributor import, existing Receivables import, and master confirmed-payment helper as nested calls. Distributor Status and Renewal are therefore written before dependent bills, and bills before dependent payments, while all remain inside the same transaction. Any typed child rejection is raised inside the outer subtransaction, rolling back the master receipt and every earlier business write before a typed failure is returned. Unexpected failures propagate and PostgreSQL rolls back the entire RPC. Exact-operation replay returns the stored response; operation reuse with another actor, request, or resolved plan is rejected.

## AUTHORIZATION AND CONCURRENCY

Admin may read all and perform manual, import, reassignment, mapping, lifecycle, billing, and renewal mutations. An assigned active employee may read its own rows and manually set that row's canonical renewal date, but cannot change any other operational fact. The server derives actor/Admin authority and database functions enforce it again. Every mutable command carries `expected_version`; stale writers receive a conflict with canonical current state. Lost-response retry reuses the stable operation ID.

## READ, WRITE, AND FAILURE BUDGET

Initial Admin load is one aggregate request for all eight cards, the global Distributor ERP footprint, and one explicit-column list request of at most 50 rows. The footprint reads only `distributor_accounts.erp_id` joined by exact FK to `erp_systems`: each distributor counts once, and a NULL official assignment is `ERP Not Set`; no Field Visit or current-baseline authority participates. One bounded canonical employee-directory read is shared with Payment Collection. There is no polling, full-table hydration, N+1 owner lookup, Realtime subscription, binary evidence, or hot `select('*')`.

The Renewals screen has a separate frozen budget: one metrics request plus one server-filtered list request of at most 50 explicit rows. A card changes the list filter without repeating metrics. The list joins the assigned employee name in the bounded server read.

Mutations may write only `distributor_accounts`, `distributor_status_events`, `distributor_operation_receipts`, and `distributor_import_batches`. ERP payment status uses the existing versioned operation receipt and status-event boundary and reads canonical financial state without writing it. They never write Leads, Pipeline, Tasks, Calls, Attendance, Field Visits, Receivables, Payments, or Chat. A zero-row result is an active empty feature. Unauthorized, capability-missing, and server-error outcomes remain typed and are never converted to an empty array.
